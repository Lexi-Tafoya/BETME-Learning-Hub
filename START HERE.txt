TO RUN THE LIVE SESSION (phones can join)
-----------------------------------------
1. Open a terminal in this folder.
2. Run:      node server.js
3. It prints the links. Open the first two on your laptop:

     FACILITATOR CONSOLE   http://localhost:8080/presenter
     PROJECTED DISPLAY     http://localhost:8080/display
     PARTICIPANTS          http://<your-ip>:8080/join     <- the QR code

4. Put the projected display on the room screen. Keep the console on your laptop.
5. Click "Live controls" -> "Show join QR" and let the room scan it once.

Phones must be on the same Wi-Fi as your laptop. Nothing is installed on phones.

You do NOT need to paste a key. Opening /presenter on the laptop running the
server claims the console and keeps it, refresh included. The terminal also
prints a /presenter?key=... link -- use that one only from a different device,
or to take control back. If a console is ever not in control, it says so on
screen rather than letting the buttons quietly do nothing.

The QR is drawn fresh every time it is requested, for whatever address the
laptop currently has. Change rooms, change Wi-Fi, it stays correct. There is
nothing to regenerate.


TO RUN WITHOUT PHONES (backup mode)
-----------------------------------
Just double-click  index.html

Everything works exactly as before: you capture the room's answers on the
laptop by tapping each option once per raised hand, or by typing counts in
facilitator mode. Use this if the network fails or you prefer manual entry.
The opening confidence question is designed to work either way.


THE FIVE THINGS TO KNOW
-----------------------
1. SPACE or Continue moves everything forward. Content unfolds one idea at a
   time and the camera flies between sections. You never say "next slide".

2. Press F for facilitator mode: your talking points, timings, handoffs and
   the live controls. Press F again for a clean screen.

3. Press M for the journey map. Click any of the twelve sections to jump.

4. Press T to start the session clock. Double-click it to reset.

5. Live controls (bottom right, facilitator console only):
     Open activity      pushes it to every phone
     Close responses    stops accepting, keeps what came in
     Reopen             lets latecomers in
     Show / Hide results
     Reveal teaching point
     Start / Pause / Reset the activity timer
     Show join QR
     Clear this activity
     Export results     anonymous aggregate JSON

PRIVACY
-------
No name, no email, no employee ID, no login, no Microsoft identity. Phones
store one random id so a re-submission replaces rather than duplicates.
Nothing is written to disk; closing the server discards everything. Written
answers appear without any attribution, and the Final Reflection is never
displayed at all.


IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
-----------------------
Participant phone slept or refreshed   It reconnects and resumes by itself.
Facilitator refresh                    Reopens on the same scene with responses intact.
Display refresh                        Re-syncs to the presenter within a second.
Live connection drops                  A notice appears; the presentation keeps
                                       working and you can capture answers on the laptop.
Need a specific scene fast             index.html?s=jordan-worksheet  (or ?s=22)
Moved to a different network           Nothing to do. Reopen the join QR.
Controls seem to do nothing            Read the banner on screen. Another device
                                       holds the console; open the
                                       /presenter?key=... link to take it back.

Every activity's presenter notes contain a no-technology backup. The full
workshop is deliverable with no phones and no server.


TIMING
------
32 scenes, 12 sections, 180 minutes. The six published agenda blocks are
unchanged. The Jordan practice (7/8/7) and the Final Reflection (10 minutes
including the silence) are protected — do not cut them.
